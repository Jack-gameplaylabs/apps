Support

Need help with Tour Born? We’re happy to assist with questions, bugs, and feedback.

Email us at:

support@gameplaylabs.org

Please start your subject line with the game name

We support several apps and games from this address, so please begin your subject with Tour Born — so we can route your message quickly. Example:

  • Tour Born — Bug: putt speed feels wrong on the 18th hole
  • Tour Born — Question: how do I restore the Tour Card?
  • Tour Born — Feedback: idea for a new course

To help us respond faster, include

  • The game version (Settings / store listing).
  • Your device model and OS version (e.g., iPhone 16, iOS 26).
  • Whether you use cloud save (Game Center or Google Play Games).
  • The season, event, and hole, if relevant.
  • A clear description of the issue — what happened and what you expected.
  • For bugs: the steps to reproduce, and a screenshot if possible.

Common questions

  • Restoring the Tour Card: use “Restore Purchases” in the game while signed in to the same store account.
  • Chests and boosts are used up when purchased, so the store does not restore them. Items you received and effects applied are kept in your progress — sign in to Game Center or Google Play Games to keep that progress if you reinstall or change devices on the same platform.
  • Moving between iPhone and Android: saved progress cannot be transferred between platforms.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play or Apple; see the Terms of Service.

Tour Born has no account or login of its own and stores nothing on our servers, so we never need a password or personal data to help you.
