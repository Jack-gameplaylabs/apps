Every shot grows your golfer

Swing with your finger and watch your golfer improve: skills, condition, shot shapes, and nerve under pressure. Ratings shape the challenge, but your hands decide the shot.

From amateur to the tour

Play amateur events and the qualifier, earn your place, then chase the majors and the world ranking against named rivals. Play the decisive shots yourself and fast-forward the rest.

Pass it on

When your golfer retires, raise the next generation your way — new strengths, a new style, and a family legacy that carries on.

How it works

No ads. Practice, amateur events, the qualifier, and your first developmental-tour season are free. The Tour Card is a one-time purchase that unlocks the main tour, the majors, and the next generation. Contains paid random items (club chests); drop rates are shown in the game before purchase.

Tour Born is intended for ages 13 and older. All golfers, tours, tournaments, and courses in the game are fictional.
