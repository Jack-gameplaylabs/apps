Tour Born

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · This edition covers availability in the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, and New Zealand.

Tour Born (“the game”, “we”) is a golf career game published by Gameplay Labs LLC. The game has no account or login of our own, no server of our own, and no ads. This policy explains how data is handled.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our own servers. Your career progress, your golfers (including any names you give them), clubs and other items you own, settings, and stats are stored on your device, and — if you use cloud save as described below — in your Apple or Google saved-game storage. We never receive them.

If you use your device’s own backup (such as iCloud Backup or Google backup), your game data may be included in that backup. Device backups are controlled by you and by Apple or Google; we do not receive or access them. The limited app-usage data processed by Google for analytics is described below.

Cloud save (Game Center and Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Game Center on iOS or Google Play Games on Android, the game can save your progress, the items you own (including items received from chests), and related counts to that service’s saved-game storage, so they can be restored when you reinstall the game or move to a new device on the same platform. To do this, the game receives a player identifier from that service and uses it only to load and save your progress. Your saved game is stored by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we do not receive it, and we do not send your Game Center or Google Play Games identity to our analytics provider. Saved games cannot be moved between Apple and Android devices.

If you are not signed in, your progress and items are stored only on your device and are lost if you delete the game. You can delete saved-game data at any time through your Game Center / iCloud settings or your Google Play Games settings.

Age information from the app store

Where the law requires it (for example, in certain U.S. states), your app store may provide the game with an age range and whether a parent or guardian has approved the game (for example, through Apple’s Declared Age Range or Google Play’s Age Signals). Tour Born uses this information only on your device to provide an age-appropriate experience and to comply with the law — for example, chests (paid random items) are not offered when the app store indicates that the user is under 16. We do not store it, send it to our analytics provider, or use it for advertising or any other purpose.

No advertising

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising software. We do not collect your device’s advertising identifier and do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Analytics

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used (for example, when a season starts and is completed, how many shots you play yourself, use of the practice range, steps on the purchase screen, when a purchase is started, completed, or restored, when a chest is opened and the rarity of the items received, and when a new generation of your golfing family begins) and to improve the game. Google processes this data on our behalf as our service provider, including app-interaction events, an app-instance identifier, coarse device information (such as device model and operating system version), and approximate region derived from IP address. Analytics does not include the names you give your golfers, age information from the app store, or your Game Center or Google Play Games identity. This analytics data is not used for advertising, and ads personalization signals are disabled. You can turn analytics off at any time in the game’s settings with “Share anonymous usage statistics”. Analytics data is retained by Google for a limited period and then deleted. See the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Purchases

The game offers optional purchases: the Tour Card (a one-time purchase), chests (paid random items), and boosts (items that speed up training or recovery). Payment is processed entirely by Google Play or Apple’s App Store through your store account; we do not receive or store your name, payment card details, or billing address. The game only receives confirmation from the store that a purchase was completed (and, where the store provides it, that a purchase was refunded), and what you have unlocked or received is stored on your device and in your cloud save if you use it. Our analytics provider records that a purchase occurred (product and price, as reported by the store) as part of app-usage statistics; this is not linked to your identity. For how Google or Apple handles payment information, see the Google Privacy Policy or Apple’s privacy policy. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live — including the United Kingdom, Australia, and New Zealand — you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, and to object to processing. Because we operate no servers and hold no personal data about you, most requests can be answered immediately: the only data processed off your device on our behalf is the app-usage data handled by our analytics provider (Google), as described above, which you can turn off at any time in the game’s settings. Saved games in Game Center or Google Play Games are held by Apple or Google in your own account and can be managed or deleted there. Information processed by Google may be transferred to and stored in the United States and other countries where Google operates. To exercise any right or raise a concern, contact privacy@gameplaylabs.org, and please include the game’s name (“Tour Born”) in the subject line so we can identify your request — we will acknowledge your message within 30 days and respond without undue delay. UK users may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Person in charge of personal information (Privacy Officer): privacy@gameplaylabs.org

Your U.S. state privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have the right to know, delete, or opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for targeted advertising. Because we do not collect or store personal information on our own servers, requests to know or delete generally concern the app-usage data processed by our analytics provider (Google) — see the Google Privacy Policy linked above. Questions: privacy@gameplaylabs.org.

Children

The game is intended for users 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If your app store indicates that a user is under 13, the game shows a notice instead of starting play, offers no purchases, and turns analytics off on that device.

Your choices

Because we store nothing on our side, you can turn off analytics in the game’s settings, delete saved-game data in your Game Center / iCloud or Google Play Games settings, and clear all game data on your device at any time by clearing the game’s storage or uninstalling it (data in your device’s own backups is managed in your device settings). The Tour Card can be restored through your store account.

Changes

If we change what data the game handles, we will update this policy and its date before the change takes effect, and tell you in the game when the change is significant.
