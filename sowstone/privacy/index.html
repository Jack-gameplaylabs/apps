Mancala 2 Player: Sowstone

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 · This edition covers availability in the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, and New Zealand.

Mancala 2 Player: Sowstone (“the game”, “Sowstone”, “we”) is published by Gameplay Labs LLC. The game has no account, no login, no server of our own, and no ads. This policy explains how data is handled.

Information we collect

We operate no servers of our own. Your games in progress, settings, and stats (such as wins and streaks) are stored on your device, and we do not receive these files. Some summary values about your play (such as the result of a game and your current winning or losing streak) are included in the analytics described below.

If you use your device’s own backup (such as iCloud Backup or Google backup), your game data may be included in that backup. Device backups are controlled by you and by Apple or Google; we do not receive or access them. The limited app-usage data processed by Google for analytics is described below.

Result images you share

When a game ends, Sowstone can create a result card (the final board, the score, the winner or a draw, the rule set, and the Sowstone name) on your device. If an image cannot be created, a short text summary is used instead. If you choose to share, it is sent only through your device’s own share feature to the app or person you pick; the game creates a temporary copy on your device for this. We do not upload, receive, or store these images, and sharing is not required to use any feature.

Age information from the app store

Where the law requires it (for example, in certain U.S. states), your app store may provide the game with an age range and whether a parent or guardian has approved the game (for example, through Apple’s Declared Age Range or Google Play’s Age Signals). Sowstone uses this information only on your device to provide an age-appropriate experience and to comply with the law; analytics stays off until this check is complete. We do not store it, send it to our analytics provider, or use it for advertising or any other purpose.

No advertising

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising software. We do not collect your device’s advertising identifier and do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Analytics

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used (for example, which screens and features are used, when a game starts, pauses, resumes, and ends, which rule set and difficulty were chosen, the result and score margin, winning and losing streaks, use of undo and hints, tips and rules viewed, settings you change, whether the share button was used, steps on the Sowstone Full Game purchase screen, how long the computer opponent takes to respond on your device, and summary values such as the number of games played, days since installation, the highest difficulty you have beaten, and whether you own Sowstone Full Game) and to improve the game. Google processes this data on our behalf as our service provider, including app-interaction events, an app-instance identifier, coarse device information (such as device model and operating system version), and approximate region derived from IP address. This analytics data is not used for advertising, and ads personalization signals are disabled. Analytics is on by default. You can turn it off at any time in the game’s settings with “Share anonymous usage statistics”, and collection stops as soon as you do. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. Analytics data is retained by Google for a limited period and then deleted. See the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Purchases

The game offers an optional one-time purchase, Sowstone Full Game (the Master computer level, Oware and additional rule sets, house-rule switches, move analysis, board themes, and stats). Payment is processed entirely by Google Play or Apple’s App Store through your store account; we do not receive or store your name, payment card details, or billing address. The game only receives confirmation from the store that the purchase was completed, and your Sowstone Full Game status is checked with the store. Our analytics provider records that a purchase occurred (product and price, as reported by the store, and on iPhone the store’s transaction reference) and whether your Sowstone Full Game status changes (for example, after a restore or a refund) as part of app-usage statistics. These records are kept with the random app-instance identifier described above and do not include your name, contact details, or payment details.

To remember your purchase, the game keeps a small record (which product you own and when it was last checked with the store) in your device’s secure storage, for this device only. On iPhone, this record may remain in the device’s keychain after you delete the game; the store remains the record of your purchase. For how Google or Apple handles payment information, see the Google Privacy Policy or Apple’s privacy policy. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live — including the United Kingdom, Australia, and New Zealand — you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, and to object to processing. We do not hold your name, contact details, or any account information. The only data processed off your device on our behalf is the app-usage data handled by our analytics provider (Google), as described above. Because it is kept with a random app-instance identifier that the game does not display, we usually cannot locate the records of a specific person; you can stop further collection at any time with the switch in the game’s settings, and we will help with any request as far as we are able. Information processed by Google may be transferred to and stored in the United States and other countries where Google operates. To exercise any right or raise a concern, contact privacy@gameplaylabs.org, and please include the game’s name (“Sowstone”) in the subject line so we can identify your request — we will acknowledge your message within 30 days and respond without undue delay. UK users may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Person in charge of personal information (Privacy Officer): privacy@gameplaylabs.org

Your U.S. state privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have the right to know, delete, or opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for targeted advertising. Because we do not collect or store personal information on our own servers, requests to know or delete generally concern the app-usage data processed by our analytics provider (Google) — see the Google Privacy Policy linked above. Questions: privacy@gameplaylabs.org.

Children

The game is intended for users 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If your app store indicates that a user is under 13, or that a parent or guardian has declined or withdrawn approval for the game, the game shows a notice instead of starting play, offers no purchases, and keeps analytics off on that device.

Your choices

You can turn off analytics in the game’s settings. Because your game data is stored on your device, you can clear it by clearing the game’s storage (Android) or uninstalling the game (data in your device’s own backups is managed in your device settings). Sowstone Full Game can be restored through your store account.

Changes

If we change what data the game handles, we will update this policy and its date before the change takes effect, and tell you in the game when the change is significant.
