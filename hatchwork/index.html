Logic, never a guess

Every puzzle has exactly one answer and can be solved line by line with logic alone. Classic black-and-white and color puzzles, from 5×5 to large grids.

Pictures worth uncovering

Puzzle pictures are adapted from public-domain natural-history plates and old prints — birds, shells, insects, plants and more — chosen by hand.

Comfortable on a phone

Drag to fill, zoom on any grid, undo as often as you like, and a color-blind-friendly palette. No timers, no lives, no energy.

Simple by design

No ads, no account, no login, no coins. 120 puzzles and a daily puzzle are free. Extra books, a Complete Set, and Unlimited Hints are optional one-time purchases, never a subscription.

Hatchwork is intended for ages 13 and older. All purchases are optional and one-time, with no subscription.
